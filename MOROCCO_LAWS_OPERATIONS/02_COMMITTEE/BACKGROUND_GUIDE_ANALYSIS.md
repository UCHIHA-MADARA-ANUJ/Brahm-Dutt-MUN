- **Stakeholder stances as the guide presents them** (simplified — chairs may expect these simplified frames; your deeper research gives you the edge but stay compatible):

  - **Russia:** rejects legally binding ban; premature; keep freedom to develop; definitions unclear.
  - **China:** supports regulation discussions; partial support for restrictions on fully autonomous lethal weapons; does NOT support limiting R&D.

  - **ICRC:** legally binding rules; prohibit systems that cannot comply with IHL; human control preserved.
  - **NGOs (HRW, Stop Killer Robots):** complete global ban.
