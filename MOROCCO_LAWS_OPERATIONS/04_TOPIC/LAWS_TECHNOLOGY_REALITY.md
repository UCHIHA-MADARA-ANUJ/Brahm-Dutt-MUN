## 2. THE SPECTRUM FRAME (your two-tier weapon)
Don't let opponents flatten this into "autonomous = evil." The spectrum:
1. **Navigation/mobility autonomy** (takeoff, loiter, return; every modern drone) → **acceptable, unregulated** — platforms.
2. **Fire-and-forget munitions** (homing missiles: decades old) → accepted; human launches.
3. **Sensor-based targeting autonomy** (loitering munitions selecting & engaging: Kargu, Harpy) → **THE contested zone — where the convention's prohibitions bite.**
4. **Fully autonomous lethal decision-making** (no human in/on loop; swarms, predictive targeting) → **prohibit — the unacceptable class.**

**Your speech line:** "Autonomy is not a switch; it is a spectrum. Morocco's position regulates the weapon function, not the platform: navigation autonomy is engineering, targeting autonomy is policy. A convention that cannot tell the difference will either ban every air force or ban nothing."
