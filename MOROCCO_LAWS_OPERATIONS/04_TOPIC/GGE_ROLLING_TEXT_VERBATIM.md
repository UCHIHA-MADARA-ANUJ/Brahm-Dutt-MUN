# 📜 GGE ROLLING TEXT — VERBATIM BOX III + "CONTEXT-APPROPRIATE HUMAN JUDGEMENT AND CONTROL" (SESSION 11)
**Sources: CCW GGE Chair's Summary (CCW-GGE.1-2025-WP.9, 20 Oct 2025), state explanation paper (CCW-GGE.1-2025-WP.4, 21 Aug 2025), CCW Report Vol. 13 No. 3 (Jun 2025). All verbatim.**

## 1. BOX III OF THE ROLLING TEXT (12 May 2025, corrected 20 Aug 2025) — the prohibitions & regulations
**These are the ACTUAL draft provisions states are negotiating. Your resolution's OP1/OP6 should mirror them.**
1. "It is prohibited in all circumstances to use LAWS which are of a nature to cause superfluous injury or unnecessary suffering, which are inherently indiscriminate, or which are otherwise incapable of being used in compliance with IHL."
2. "LAWS may only be used in accordance with IHL, including the principles and requirements of distinction, proportionality and precautions in attack."
3. "It is prohibited in all circumstances to make the civilian population as such, individual civilians or civilian objects the object of an attack by LAWS."
4. "It is prohibited to use LAWS if their effects in attack cannot be anticipated and controlled, as required by IHL in the circumstances of their use."
5. "The prohibitions set forth in the preceding paragraphs must be taken into account in the design, development, and use of LAWS."
6. "In order to uphold compliance with IHL, States should ensure context-appropriate human judgement and control in the use of LAWS, through, inter alia, the following measures:
   - (a) Ensure the effects of LAWS are adequately predictable, reliable, traceable and explainable to those responsible for their use.
   - (b) Ensure that LAWS are operated under a responsible chain of command and control. This includes ensuring assessment of legal obligations and ethical considerations by a human, in particular, with regard to the effects of the selection and engagement functions.
   - (c) Ensure that LAWS' mission parameters with regard to the types of targets, duration, geographical scope, and scale of the operation cannot be modified by the system without context-appropriate human judgement and control.
   - (d) Control, limit or otherwise restrict the types of targets, duration, geographical scope, and scale of the operation of LAWS, including through, as appropriate:
     - (i) Ensuring that LAWS can be deactivated in a timely manner and/or incorporate self-destruct [mechanisms]..."

**Debate uses:**
- "The rolling text's own Box III prohibits inherently indiscriminate systems, prohibits attacking civilians, prohibits effects that cannot be anticipated and controlled — and requires context-appropriate human judgement and control. The draft law already exists. The only question is whether it stays a text or becomes a treaty."
- Your OP6 verbatim upgrade: borrow paragraphs 1, 3, 4, 6 directly.

## 2. WHAT "CONTEXT-APPROPRIATE HUMAN JUDGEMENT AND CONTROL" MEANS (WP.4, submitting states)
- (a) **"Context-appropriate"** = "the level and type of human involvement necessary considering the circumstances, such as the operational environment and conditions, type of weapons, and mission planned"
- (b) **"Human judgement"** = "humans exercising their judgement including in relation to compliance with rules and principles of IHL, in particular distinction, precautions, and proportionality when launching an attack"
- (c) **"Control"** = "having sufficient assurance that weapons systems, once activated, act in a foreseeable manner in order to determine that their effects are in conformity with applicable international law, in particular IHL, rules of engagement, and the intentions of their commanders and operators"
- ⭐ **The clarification that kills the "you want to ban all autonomy" attack:** "Human judgement and control do not in any way imply a universal rule to ensure direct control or direct oversight during the use of a weapon system as this is not required by IHL." (WP.4, para 5)
- **Art. 36 hook:** "for those states that ratified Additional Protocol I..., context-appropriate human judgment and control is exercised indirectly through the legal review process, as set out in Art. 36"
- **Proportionality requires human judgement** (qualitative + quantitative assessment of military advantage vs collateral damage)
- **Control includes:** retaining human approval for any substantial modification of mission parameters; assessing whether communication links remain required; ability to deactivate (safety stop)
- **Debate uses:**
  - "The states that drafted 'context-appropriate human judgement and control' explicitly said it does NOT require direct control at all times. IHL does not require a human hand on every trigger. What it requires is humans setting, supervising and terminating — a standard every modern military already meets for lawful systems. The objection that this bans all autonomy is a misreading of the text the objector signed up to discuss."
  - Your OP4 definitional work = WP.4's (a)/(b)/(c) almost verbatim.

## 3. THE JUDGMENT vs CONTROL DISTINCTION (South Africa, Germany, ICRC, Ireland — CCW Report Vol. 13 No. 3)
- **South Africa:** "context-appropriate human control relates to the direct operation and engagement with the weapon system, to ensure that human oversight is maintained throughout the targeting process. In contrast... human judgement involves a higher level of decision-making, including determining when, how, and under what conditions LAWS may be employed."
- **Germany:** "human control implies not only technical control, but also an element of judgment... human judgment will be applied within the responsible chain of command."
- **ICRC:** "context-appropriate human control and judgment would not require direct physical control of the weapon system at all times, but requires instead that relevant control is maintained over the effects of the weapon system... established through a combination of prohibitions and restrictions... in Box III."
- **Ireland:** "autonomy is a spectrum... those that fail to meet [the Box III] criteria—which is clearly defined...—should be prohibited, and the rest should be subject to measures and restrictions."
- **UK (at the same session):** "human control is not a deliberate requirement of IHL. However... human control is essential to ensure compliance with IHL" — **use this against the US/UK: even the UK concedes control is ESSENTIAL even if not a formal IHL requirement.**
- **Debate uses:** the "spectrum" framing (Ireland) + "control over effects, not direct handling" (ICRC) = your two-tier's technical spine.

## 4. THE "IHL IS ENOUGH" COUNTER (final form)
1. UK's own concession: control is "essential to ensure compliance" — policy, not law.
2. ICRC: instrument must go beyond restating IHL → "additional specific measures."
3. Rolling text Box III: already contains prohibitions + regulations (i.e., the "additional specific measures" drafted).
4. NAM/Arab Group/African Group: binding instrument demanded since 2020-2023.
5. Conclusion line: "The states that say IHL is enough are the same states that say the rolling text is too much. If IHL were enough, the text would be unnecessary; if the text is unnecessary, why do they fight it paragraph by paragraph?"
