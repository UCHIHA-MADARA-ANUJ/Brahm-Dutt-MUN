## 1. THE CCW SYSTEM (your home turf — know it cold)
- `[VERIFIED]` **Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW, 1980, in force 1983)** — "umbrella" convention, 128 States parties (Oct 2025). Covers weapons deemed excessively injurious or indiscriminate. **Morocco is a State party.**
- **Structure:** framework convention + annexed protocols. A State party must accept at least two protocols:
  - **Protocol I** — non-detectable fragments (ban)
  - **Protocol II / Amended Protocol II (1996)** — mines, booby-traps (restrictions; allows marked/controlled minefields with self-destruct standards; scope extended to internal conflicts)
  - **Protocol III** — incendiary weapons (restrictions)
  - **Protocol IV (1995)** — blinding laser weapons (ban — the model for LAWS: negotiated BEFORE the weapon was entrenched)
  - **Protocol V (2003)** — explosive remnants of war (clearance obligations)
- **Key structural facts for debate:**
  - CCW has **no verification or enforcement mechanism** and no formal compliance process (a state can withdraw on 1 year's notice) — your answer to "how is it enforced?": national implementation + review conferences + political cost; a Protocol VI would inherit this structure (and that's exactly why you pair it with national legal-review obligations).
  - **Protocol IV is your historical precedent:** states banned blinding lasers in 1995 *before* they were widespread. Use it: "We banned blinding lasers before they blinded a generation. We can ban uncontrollable autonomy before it kills a generation."
  - CCW operates **by consensus** — the blocker's weapon. Your answer: "Consensus is a result, not a method. Ten years produced elements; the Review Conference must produce a mandate."

## 2. IHL — THE SUBSTANTIVE LAW THAT ALREADY APPLIES
- **Geneva Conventions (1949) + Additional Protocol I (1977, art. 35–36, 48–58):** distinction, proportionality, precaution in attack; **Art. 36 legal review of new weapons** (states must determine whether new weapons violate IHL — YOUR OP5's legal basis).
- **Martens Clause:** in cases not covered by treaty, civilians and combatants remain under "principles of humanity and dictates of public conscience" — the maximalists' favorite; use it for the anti-personnel agenda: "the public conscience has already answered."
- **The IHL problem with LAWS (your core argument):** IHL was written for human judgment (distinction/proportionality require *judgment*); an opaque, unpredictable system may be *incapable* of compliance — hence "a law that applies to everyone and binds no one protects no one."
- `[VERIFIED]` **ICRC position (March 2026):** new instrument must (a) **prohibit** unpredictable/opaque AWS and **anti-personnel AWS** (designed or used to target persons); (b) **restrict** all others (limits on targets; duration/scale/geographic scope; situations of use; supervision; deactivation). **This is the ICRC's own two-tier — cite it when attacked for "inventing" two-tier.**
