## 2. KEY VERBATIM EVOLUTIONS (borrow the latest form — 80/57 — but cite the lineage)
**Applicable law (grew over time):**
- 78/241: "international law, in particular the Charter... international humanitarian law and international human rights law, applies"

- 80/57: back to "the Charter..., international humanitarian law and international human rights law"
- **Use:** the criminal-law element is in 79/62 — your accountability OP5 can cite it: "the Assembly has already affirmed that international criminal law applies. Accountability is not a future question; it is an existing obligation."

**The two-tier (appeared 2024, reaffirmed 2025):**
- 79/62 PP: "Noting the repeated calls by the Secretary-General to urgently conclude negotiations of a legally binding instrument with prohibitions and regulations for autonomous weapons systems, in line with a two-tier approach"
- 80/57 PP: "Noting the calls by the Secretary-General to commence negotiations of a legally binding instrument..., in line with a two-tier approach of prohibitions and regulations"
- **Use:** "The two-tier approach is not a Moroccan invention. It is in the Assembly's own resolutions, twice."

## 4. SG'S MAY 2025 CONSULTATIONS MESSAGE (verbatim, primary UN source)
From the Secretary-General's video message, informal consultations on LAWS, New York, 12 May 2025:
- "**Machines that have the power and discretion to take human lives without human control are politically unacceptable, morally repugnant and should be banned by international law.**"
- "I reiterate my call for the conclusion of a legally binding instrument by 2026."
- "**Human control over the use of force is essential. We cannot delegate life-or-death decisions to machines.**"
- "Time is running out to take preventative action."
- "On an issue of such concern to all of humanity, every voice must be heard."
- **Use:** the "politically unacceptable, morally repugnant" line is the guide's own cited resource; deploy it in the opening or the ban-vs-regulate debate. Source: https://www.un.org/sg/en/content/sg/statement/2025-05-12/secretary-generals-video-message-the-informal-consultations-lethal-autonomous-weapons-systems
