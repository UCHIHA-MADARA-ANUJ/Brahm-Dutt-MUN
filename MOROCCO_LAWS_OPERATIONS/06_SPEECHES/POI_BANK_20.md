# 🎯 POI BANK — 20 STRATEGIC QUESTIONS (SESSION 6)
**Rules of deployment:** 1) Never ask what you don't know the answer to (you must be ready to rebut the reply). 2) Ask to expose, not to humiliate. 3) Timing: ask after the target's speech, during POI windows, or in moderated caucus directed questions. 4) Two or three well-placed POIs per session is elite; ten is noise.

## TARGETING THE VOLUNTARY-FIRST BLOC (US/Japan/ROK-leaning)
1. **To US:** "The distinguished delegate's own Political Declaration includes legal reviews, testing and accountability measures. If those measures are sound enough to volunteer, why are they not sound enough to universalize as binding obligations?" *(Forces them to argue against their own text.)*
2. **To US:** "In 2023 the United States said the time was not right to begin negotiations. What has changed in three years — the technology, or the politics?"
3. **To any voluntary-first state:** "Your national measures bind your current administration. What binds the next one?"
4. **To Japan/ROK-leaning:** "Japan and the Republic of Korea host the REAIM summits precisely because the military AI race needs rules. Does the delegation believe the REAIM Blueprint should remain a blueprint forever?"
5. **To any NATO-leaning abstainer:** "Which element of the GGE rolling text does your delegation reject — the characterization, human control, or accountability? And what is your alternative text?"

## TARGETING THE BLOCKERS (Russia/Belarus/DPRK)
6. **To Russia:** "The Russian Federation says the CCW is the right forum — Morocco agrees, and our resolution sends the mandate there. Will the Russian Federation commit, in this committee, not to block a negotiation mandate at the Review Conference?"
7. **To Russia:** "You cite the absence of an agreed definition as the obstacle. The GGE has a working characterization, and definitions are precisely what negotiations produce. What definition does your delegation propose?"
8. **To any blocker:** "If consensus in the CCW means one state can delay law indefinitely, what is the delegate's alternative mechanism for the 130 states that want a treaty?"

## TARGETING THE MAXIMALISTS (Mexico/Costa Rica/Pakistan)
9. **To maximalist:** "Your text prohibits all autonomous systems. Does it also prohibit autonomous missile-defense systems and autonomous navigation? If not, where exactly is the line — and who draws it?"
10. **To maximalist:** "A ban without a negotiation mandate is a gesture; a ban inside a negotiated instrument is law. Will you join a text that puts the anti-personnel prohibition at the top of the negotiation agenda, with a date?"
11. **To Pakistan:** "Pakistan has been the conscience of this debate since the Protocol VI draft. The question for the conscience: does a perfect text that is never ratified protect more civilians than a strong text that enters into force next year?"

## TARGETING THE PRODUCERS (Türkiye/China/India/UK)
12. **To Türkiye (private, not formal):** "Ankara has said weapons without meaningful human control conflict with IHL. Our OP4 codifies exactly that standard. Will Türkiye help us define it — and join the negotiation mandate that protects the lawful industry we both build?" *(Do NOT ask this in open forum — it invites a defensive answer.)*
13. **To China:** "China's five criteria for prohibited systems are the most detailed proposal on the table. Would China support placing its criteria — alongside the ICRC framework — on the negotiation agenda of the instrument?"
14. **To China:** "If use is prohibited but development is not, how does the delegation verify that a system 'will not be used' in the prohibited mode — and does that verification exist in any national law?"
15. **To India:** "India has emphasized meaningful human control and gradual regulation. Does the delegation consider eleven years of GGE discussion 'gradual' — and if not, what timeline does it propose?"
16. **To UK:** "The United Kingdom conducts legal reviews of weapons systems and has published autonomy doctrine. Would the UK support making such reviews a binding obligation for all States parties to the future instrument?"

## TARGETING THE SWING/AFRICAN MIDDLE
17. **To any abstainer:** "Your delegation abstained on resolution 80/57. Which paragraph caused the abstention — the concern about proliferation to non-State actors, the human-control language, or the call to complete the elements?"
18. **To Saudi Arabia/Gulf-leaning:** "The Arab Group has supported a binding instrument. Would the delegation support the capacity-building clause that guarantees developing States a place at the negotiation table — the clause that makes the treaty ours, not theirs?"
19. **To any small state:** "If the convention is negotiated only by the states that already have the expertise, will your delegation be at the table? Our OP9 answers that question — does the delegate's?"
20. **To Algeria (do this in unmod, not public POI):** "Algeria and Morocco have voted identically on every LAWS resolution. Shall we co-draft the African amendments so the continent's voice is one voice — on this agenda?"

## THE POI FORMULA (delivery)
- **Format:** "A point of information to the distinguished delegate of ___ — [one sentence, one question]."
- **Never:** compound questions, rhetorical questions you can't answer, personal attacks, questions answered by "we'll decide in negotiations" (unless that's the trap).
- **After their answer:** one follow-up max ("And if [X], then [Y]?") or a thank-you. Never argue in the POI window — save it for the next speech.
