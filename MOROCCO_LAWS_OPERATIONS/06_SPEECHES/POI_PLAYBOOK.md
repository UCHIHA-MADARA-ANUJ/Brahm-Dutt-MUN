# 🎯 POI PLAYBOOK — HOW TO RAISE POIs THAT SCORE (SESSION 9)
**Combined with POI_BANK_20.md (the 20 questions). This file = mechanics + 12 more questions + phase timing.**

## 1. THE MECHANICS (know your conference's rule — confirm day 1)
- **During a speech (if POIs allowed):** raise placard when the speaker/chair invites POIs; the chair recognizes you; stand and say: *"Point of information to the distinguished delegate of ___."* The speaker may accept or decline (many rules let them decline — don't take it personally).
- **If POIs are NOT allowed during speeches:** use moderated-caucus "directed questions" — motion a caucus on the topic and ask from the floor, or ask in unmod (private or group).
- **After a speech (yield to questions):** if the speaker yields to questions, this is YOUR window — be first.
- **Format:** ONE question, ONE sentence, ends with "?" Never compound. Never a statement disguised as a question. Never "doesn't the delegate agree that..." (that's a speech).
- **When recognized:** stand straight, name yourself ("Delegation of Morocco"), ask, sit. Don't argue the answer in the window — follow up in your NEXT speech.

## 2. THE TIMING (when a POI scores vs when it backfires)
| Phase | Best POI target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| After opening GSLs | The loudest maximalist | Establish your synthesis lane early: "which systems exactly does your text prohibit?" |
| After a rival's strong speech | Their weakest clause | "Your OP on verification — who pays, who monitors, by when?" |
| Before a vote | The abstainer | "Which paragraph caused the abstention?" — forces them to justify publicly |
| During crisis | The blocker | "What is your alternative mechanism for the 130 states?" |
| NEVER | After your own allies' weak speeches | Don't damage your bloc; whisper fixes in unmod instead |

## 3. THE 12 NEW QUESTIONS (phase-targeted)
**On the "ban everything" maximalist (Mexico/Costa Rica/Pakistan):**
1. "The delegate's text prohibits all autonomous systems. Does it prohibit autonomous missile-defense? If not, what is the line, and who draws it in law?"
2. "A moratorium without a negotiation mandate is a gesture. Will the delegate join a text that pairs the anti-personnel prohibition with a date?"
**On the voluntary-first bloc (US/UK/Japan/ROK/Australia/Canada):**
3. "The United Kingdom's own submission says there is no gap in IHL — yet also requires meaningful human control across all use cases. If human control is required, what enforces it — a policy of the day, or law?"
4. "Canada says 'human involvement' means both human judgment and human control. What does Canada propose to make that standard universal rather than national?"
5. "Japan and the Republic of Korea co-lead the REAIM process. Does the delegation believe REAIM's Blueprint should remain a voluntary blueprint forever?"
6. "Australia is a CCW party. What is Australia's alternative text to the rolling text's human-control provisions?"
**On Russia/blockers:**
7. "The Russian Federation says definitions are unclear. The GGE has a working characterization and the ICRC has a framework. Which definition does Russia propose instead of none?"
8. "If consensus means one state can delay law indefinitely, what mechanism does the delegate propose for the 130 states that want a treaty?"
**On the producers (Türkiye/China/India):**
9. "Türkiye has stated that weapons without meaningful human control conflict with IHL. Does Türkiye therefore support codifying that standard in the negotiation mandate?" *(Ask only if Türkiye's delegate is receptive; otherwise private.)*
10. "China's five criteria prohibit systems that are lethal, fully autonomous, non-terminable, indiscriminate and self-evolving. Does China agree these criteria belong on the negotiation agenda?"
**On swing states (Saudi/Gulf/Poland/Japan):**
11. "Saudi Arabia is a member of the Arab Group, which supports a binding instrument. What would move Saudi Arabia from abstention to support — the capacity-building clause, or the timeline?"
12. "The delegate abstained on resolution 80/57. In one sentence: which paragraph was the obstacle — and is it in this draft?"

## 4. THE FOLLOW-UP RULE
- After their answer: either "I thank the delegate" (if they answered) or ONE follow-up max: "And if [X], then [Y]?" — then stop.
- **Never** turn the POI window into a debate. The debate is your next speech, where you quote their answer: *"The distinguished delegate confirmed that [X] — which is precisely why this committee must [Y]."*

## 5. THE POI SCOREBOARD (what the dais notes)
- **+1** precise, single-sentence question that exposes a gap
- **+1** question that uses a verified fact (numbers, dates, quotes)
- **+1** follow-up that lands the point without arguing
- **-2** compound questions, gotcha theater, attacking a person, asking what you can't rebut
