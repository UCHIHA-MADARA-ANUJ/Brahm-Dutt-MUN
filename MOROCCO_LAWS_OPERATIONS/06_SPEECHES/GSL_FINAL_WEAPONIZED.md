## THE SPEECH

**[0:00–0:14 — AUTHORITY. Calm. Feet planted. Do not touch the paper.]**

Mr. President, distinguished delegates —

The Kingdom of Morocco speaks as a State party to the CCW, a member of the African Group that declared itself **ready** to negotiate, and a nation that operates modern drone technology under strict human command. ⏸

**[0:14–0:22 — THE VERDICT. Drop volume. 🔉]**

🔉 We were asked to *evaluate* the need for a binding convention. 🐢 Evaluation ended long ago. Our answer is a verdict.

**[0:22–0:48 — THE STRIKE WINDOW. Build with each number.]**

In December, 🐢 **one hundred and sixty-four states** — Morocco among them — demanded the completion of the elements for this instrument. One hundred and thirty support negotiating a treaty. The African Group said in March: *we are ready and committed.*

👁 [US → Russia seats] And six states voted no. ⏸ 🔊 **Six.** This Assembly will not schedule humanity around the convenience of six. ⏸

**[0:48–0:56 — THE FRESH WOUND. Back to 80%. Evidence voice.]**

👁 [sweep the room] This month, reports from Ukraine describe drone swarms selecting and killing human beings with **no human decision at all**. 🔉 The only thing still hypothetical... is the law.

**[0:56–1:18 — THE TEXT. You are the drafter. Steady, fast-ish, confident.]**

Morocco proposes three commitments. **One:** a negotiation mandate at the November Review Conference — and if Geneva cannot decide, this Assembly convenes the conference in 2027. **Two:** prohibit weapons that select and engage targets without meaningful human control; regulate all others. **Three:** Africa and the developing world enter as co-authors — capacity, participation, access.

**[1:18–1:30 — THE CLOSE. 🐢🔉 Slow. Quiet. This is the line they write down.]**

🔉 We do not need more analysis. 🐢 We need a calendar. ⏸ Morocco has brought one — and a pen for every delegation ready to sign. Thank you.
