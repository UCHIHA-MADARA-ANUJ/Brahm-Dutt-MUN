# 🎙️ LEGENDARY QUOTES — THE LINES THAT DEFINE THIS DEBATE (SESSION 11)
**Deploy sparingly but memorably. Each is verbatim with source. A perfect quote at the right moment is a Best-Delegate moment.**

## 1. CHRISTOF HEYNS (UN Special Rapporteur, report A/HRC/23/47, 9 April 2013 — the origin of the debate)
- "Machines lack morality and mortality, and many people believe they should as a result not have life and death powers over humans. **This is among the reasons landmines were banned.**"
- "Decisions over life and death in armed conflict may require compassion and intuition."
- "The matter will, quite literally, be taken out of human hands." (if nothing is done)
- "It is an underlying assumption of most legal, moral and other codes that when the decision to take life or to subject people to other grave consequences is at stake, the [decision must be made by a human]."
- Heyns was SOUTH AFRICAN — **your African-Group pride card:** "This debate began with a South African voice. Africa gave the world the warning; the world owes Africa the law."
- **Source:** OHCHR story (31 May 2013); HRW "Heed the Call" (21 Aug 2018); UK Hansard (17 Jun 2013).

## 2. UN SECRETARY-GENERAL (May 2025, NY consultations)
- "**Machines that have the power and discretion to take human lives without human control are politically unacceptable, morally repugnant and should be banned by international law.**"
- "Human control over the use of force is essential. We cannot delegate life-or-death decisions to machines."
- "Time is running out to take preventative action."
- **Source:** un.org/sg statement, 12 May 2025.

## 3. SG REPORT A/79/88 (July 2024)

## 4. AUSTRIAN FM SCHALLENBERG (Vienna 2024)

## 5. THE FIRST COMMITTEE 2023 SESSION WARNING

## 6. THE "SLAUGHTERBOTS" FRAME (IEEE Spectrum, 2022 — the public-conscience line)
- The Kargu-2: "a Slaughterbot" — mass-produced, fully autonomous targeting, facial recognition, swarm-capable, works under jamming.
- **Use with care** — civil-society language in committee can sound NGO-ish; convert to: "The public conscience has already named these systems. The Martens Clause requires us to hear it."

## 7. THE MARTENS CLAUSE (your maximalist-courtship legal anchor)
- "Until a more complete code of the laws of war has been issued, the High Contracting Parties deem it expedient to declare that, in cases not included in the Regulations adopted by them, **the inhabitants and the belligerents remain under the protection and the rule of the principles of the law of nations, as they result from the usages established among civilized peoples, from the laws of humanity, and the dictates of the public conscience.**"
- **Use:** "The dictates of the public conscience — the same clause that guided the blinding-lasers ban and the landmine ban — have already answered whether machines may decide who lives."
