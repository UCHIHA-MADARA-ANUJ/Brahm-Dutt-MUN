## 1. UNITED STATES
- **Position `[VERIFIED]`:** IHL generally sufficient; opposes complete ban; prefers "appropriate levels of human judgment" over "meaningful human control"; prefers non-binding guidelines (Political Declaration on Responsible Military Use of AI, Nov 2023); voted **NO** on A/RES/80/57 (2025) and 79/62? (No — US voted YES on 79/62 in 2024, moved to NO in 2025; verify in-room). DoD Directive 3000.09 governs its autonomy.
- **What they want:** preserve military AI lead; no negotiation mandate; their declaration as the framework; innovation protection.
- **Their price (what could soften them):** preambular recognition of the Political Declaration; OP7 transparency language (their own content); legal-review obligations (they already practice); no anti-personnel ban pre-judged.
- **Approach script:** "We are not asking the US to abandon its declaration — we are asking it to be the first signatory of its own standards as law. Your ten measures are our implementation chapter."
- **Attack on Morocco:** "Morocco is America's partner yet votes against our position" / "Morocco endorses our declaration then undermines it."
- **Your defense:** "Endorsing implementation measures has never meant accepting substitutes. Our three yes votes are consistent with our declaration membership: we implement what we endorse."
- **Recruitment verdict:** HARD OPPOSITION as leader of the voluntary bloc — do not expect a yes; isolate with recorded votes + OP7 trap. Expect their delegate to be strong; attack policy, not person.

## 2. RUSSIA
- **Position `[VERIFIED]`:** rejects legally binding ban; "premature"; definitions unclear; freedom to develop military tech; voted NO on 78/241, 79/62, 80/57; blocks CCW consensus.
- **What they want:** nothing binding; narrow definitions; keep CCW as a veto-zone; avoid isolation (they'll claim "consensus protects everyone").
- **Approach:** rhetorical isolation only. Frame: "One state's veto in Geneva cannot veto humanity's need for law." Never trade substance for their support.
- **Attack on Morocco:** "Morocco follows the Western agenda" (standard Russian play).
- **Defense:** "Morocco votes with 164 states and with the African Group. If that is a bloc, it is the bloc of the majority of humanity."
- **Recruitment verdict:** HARD OPPOSITION / BLOCKER. Ignore for substance; use as the foil.

## 3. CHINA
- **Position `[VERIFIED]`:** two-tier framework! Prohibits "unacceptable" systems defined by **five cumulative criteria**: (1) lethal, (2) fully autonomous with no intervention possible, (3) incapable of termination, (4) indiscriminate effects, (5) uncontrolled evolution/self-learning. Regulates all others with "appropriate human involvement." Wants CCW as THE forum; does NOT support limiting R&D; abstains on GA resolutions (2023, 2024, 2025); supports negotiations on regulation; opposes use of fully autonomous lethal weapons ("ban on use only").
- **What they want:** CCW primacy; no R&D restrictions; narrow prohibition scope; US-China tech parity.
- **Their price:** explicit CCW-first structure (our OP2 does this); OP10 savings clause for R&D/civilian AI; acknowledgment of their five-criteria proposal in the negotiation agenda.
- **Approach script:** "China's five criteria and the two-tier approach share the same architecture: prohibit the unacceptable, regulate the rest. We are not asking China to abandon its proposal — we are asking it to help us define 'unacceptable' together in negotiations."
- **Attack on Morocco:** unlikely directly; may push "African states should follow AU/China positions" subtly.
- **Recruitment verdict:** SOFT OPPOSITION → potential abstention-not-opposition or even yes on a CCW-first text. Recruit via OP2 + OP10 + five-criteria acknowledgment.
