# PART 19 — CONTINGENCY PLANS (A–J)

- **PLAN A (everything goes well):** You draft the reference text in unmod #1; Bloc A unifies around it; Bloc B trades its implementation chapter for mandate language; resolution passes with 120+; you are named among lead sponsors; Review Conference mentions it in its outcome. *Keep doing:* drafting, bridging, tracking commitments.
- **PLAN B (your bloc is weak):** Austria/Brazil delegation is weak or absent. *Move:* rebuild around Sierra Leone + African Group + NAM core; use the African Group statement as the textual anchor; recruit 2–3 swing states (Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Poland) with clause offers; simplify the resolution to 3 OPs.
- **PLAN C (a major rival takes leadership):** US delegate leads a strong Bloc B. *Move:* attack the policy, not the delegate: "their own ten measures" framing; force recorded votes on the mandate OP; shift the battleground to the fallback clause (they cannot oppose a conference without opposing their own transparency agenda); offer them the implementation chapter — publicly.
- **PLAN D (your key proposal is rejected):** OP 2 (Review Conference mandate) defeated. *Move:* immediately move the fallback OP (diplomatic conference) as an amendment; reframe: "the door was closed in Geneva; we open another"; secure at least the human-control standard so the resolution still has substance; declare the partial victory loudly in explanation of vote.
- **PLAN E (you get attacked publicly):** Follow Part 12 matrix; acknowledge-reject-context-policy-redirect; use right of reply max once; never get personal; then *change the subject* by moving a caucus on your strongest subtopic (capacity building).
- **PLAN F (surprise crisis/topic shift):** Chair introduces a crisis (e.g., reported autonomous drone strike in an ongoing conflict). *Move:* integrate it as evidence: "this is why we need the calendar"; propose an emergency OP (call for immediate national moratoria on autonomous targeting pending the instrument); offer Morocco's stance: moratorium on systems operating without human control — consistent with your doctrine; keep the resolution structure.
- **PLAN G (you lose control of drafting):** Another bloc tables first. *Move:* don't fight the draft — fight the structure: propose merger; bring your three packages as the framework; get named as co-drafter; if excluded, table amendments that import your OPs (you become the amendment-maker — still central).
- **PLAN H (you must compromise):** Sequence of concessions: (1) timeline 2027/2028; (2) anti-personnel language softened to "negotiation agenda"; (3) fallback clause made conditional; (4) capacity-building fund made "invites contributions" only. Never concede: mandate existence, human control, two-tier, inclusivity, no singling-out.
- **PLAN I (you must become opposition):** If a maximalist "ban everything now" text dominates, oppose it as the drafter of the realistic alternative: "this text will be negotiated by no one and ratified by fewer"; propose the two-tier substitute; if it fails, vote no with explanation: "Morocco votes for law, not for slogans."
- **PLAN J (you become the mediator):** If Bloc A and B deadlock on wording, position yourself as the honest broker with *draft text in hand* for each disputed phrase (mandate: "calls upon... to adopt a negotiation mandate"; voluntary: "recognizing complementary national and voluntary measures, the Assembly..."; timeline: "with a view to concluding by 2028, with an interim review in 2027"). Mediation with text is power.
