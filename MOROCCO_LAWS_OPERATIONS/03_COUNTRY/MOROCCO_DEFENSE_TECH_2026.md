### The preemptive defense (add to ATTACK_MATRIX as Attack #1d)
1. **Acknowledge + classify:** "Morocco is building **defensive** autonomous systems — anti-drone defense. There is a difference between a system that intercepts incoming munitions and a system that selects and engages persons. The first is law enforcement and self-defense; the second is the object of our convention."
2. **The Iron Dome argument:** "Defensive autonomy already exists and is accepted — Iron Dome, automated naval point-defense, counter-drone systems. No delegation in this room proposes banning defensive interception. What we propose banning is autonomous targeting of persons without human control. Morocco's anti-drone systems fall on the lawful side of that line — and we are asking the world to draw that line in law."
3. **Redirect:** "The delegation attacking Morocco for building defense while supporting the treaty has confused self-defense with aggression. The convention does not disarm the defender; it disarms the automaton."
4. **Counterattack:** "Every state in this room with a modern military is building AI-enabled defense. The question is not who builds; it is what the law permits to be built. That is precisely why we need the negotiation — to define the line that separates Morocco's anti-drone system from an autonomous killer."

## 3. THE "DEFENSIVE AUTONOMY" FRAME (your structural answer to every tech attack)
Build the spectrum in debate:
1. **Defensive autonomous systems** (anti-drone, missile defense, point-defense) — lawful, existing, protecting life → **regulate, don't prohibit.**
2. **Offensive autonomous targeting of objects/materiel** (loitering anti-radar munitions with human launch) — regulated → **human control + limits on scope/duration.**
3. **Offensive autonomous targeting of persons without meaningful human control** — **prohibit.** The ICRC line, the SG's "moral line," the African Group's line, Malawi's line, Costa Rica's line.
**Your line:** "The spectrum is not between autonomy and no autonomy — it is between autonomy that protects and autonomy that kills. The convention must tell the difference. Morocco's text does."
