## 4.2 What this record means — the three-layer strategy
- **Layer 1 — PUBLIC POSITION (say openly):** Morocco supports a legally binding instrument on autonomous weapons; supports the two-tier approach (prohibitions + regulations); meaningful human control is the indispensable standard; the GGE rolling text is the negotiation basis; Africa must be a full participant; the CCW Review Conference must launch negotiations; voluntary measures (political declaration, REAIM, national legal reviews) are useful *interim* steps, not substitutes.
- **Layer 2 — NEGOTIATING POSITION (compromise space):** accept a phased negotiation (mandate 2026 → text by 2028); accept "working characterization" instead of a rigid definition; accept that prohibitions cover (i) systems functioning without meaningful human control and (ii) systems incapable of IHL compliance, with the anti-personnel prohibition question left to negotiation *if* the bloc demands it; accept transparency/confidence-building measures as the first legally binding layer; accept a review conference mechanism over standing verification.
- **Layer 3 — STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE (what you actually want):** (a) a negotiation mandate that starts before the window closes, in a forum where African states are present (CCW first, UNGA-convened conference as fallback); (b) international rules that legitimize *human-controlled* armed drones while delegitimizing autonomous targeting — this is the ideal outcome for a state that operates drones against non-state actors; (c) African capacity-building and technology access so the rules do not become a Western cartel against the Global South; (d) your own elevation as the African drafter/bridge — the best-delegate path.
