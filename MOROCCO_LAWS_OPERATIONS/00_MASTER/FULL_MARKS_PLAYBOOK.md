## 3. MODERATED CAUCUS (target ≥0.85/1)
- **Be the one who MOTIONS:** motioning a topic and then speaking to it = double visibility.
- **Get called:** sit in the chair's sightline (front-center or the side the chair looks at); placard up the instant the topic is announced; keep it up (don't wave).
- **Speak EARLY in the caucus** (first 3 speakers = sets the frame) and again if time allows with a synthesis ("we have heard two views; here is the third path").
- **Never speak twice without adding** a new fact, a new country, or a new clause.

- **Day 1 = build** (identity + bloc + draft). **Day 2 = close** (merge + amendments + vote + narrative). Winners control the transition between the two.
- Sleep ≥6h. A rested brain speaks at 8.0; a tired brain speaks at 5.1.
