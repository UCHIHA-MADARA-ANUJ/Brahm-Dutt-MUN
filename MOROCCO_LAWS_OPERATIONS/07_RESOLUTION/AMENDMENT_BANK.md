# 🛠️ AMENDMENT BANK — PRE-DRAFTED (T-1, SESSION 4)
**Use:** when the rival bloc tables its text, or when your draft faces hostile amendments. Each amendment is a complete, tappable motion. Know when to deploy (Column: Trigger).

---

## A. FRIENDLY AMENDMENTS (to YOUR OWN draft — strengthen before rivals do)

### FA-1: Add anti-personnel prohibition to the negotiation agenda
- **Target:** after OP1 (insert new OP1bis).
- **Text:** *"Decides that the negotiations referred to in operative paragraph 2 shall address, as a priority, the question of prohibiting autonomous weapons systems designed or used to apply force against persons, in accordance with the recommendations of the International Committee of the Red Cross and consistent with existing international humanitarian law;"*
- **Purpose:** buys the maximalist wing (Mexico, Costa Rica, Malawi, Pakistan, HRW-aligned delegates) into your text without pre-judging the outcome. Your public line: "we put it on the table; the negotiation decides."
- **Trigger:** if maximalists threaten a rival "ban everything" draft.

### FA-2: Producers' and operators' consultative forum
- **Target:** after OP9 (insert OP9bis).
- **Text:** *"Requests the Secretary-General to establish, within existing resources, an open consultative forum of States that produce, operate or export autonomous weapons systems and related technologies, to inform the negotiation process with technical and operational expertise, ensuring the participation of developing States;"*
- **Purpose:** Türkiye's price + China/ROK/UK softener + legitimizes Morocco's operator status. Nobody can vote against "a seat at the table."
- **Trigger:** during unmod, when recruiting Türkiye/UK/Japan.

### FA-3: Interim national moratorium (crisis/first-day urgency move)
- **Target:** after OP6 (insert OP6bis).
- **Text:** *"Calls upon all States, pending the entry into force of the instrument, to refrain from the development and use of autonomous weapons systems that select and engage targets without meaningful human control, and to declare such measures nationally in the spirit of interim self-restraint;"*
- **Purpose:** answers the "what do we do NOW" question; gives you a dramatic first-day proposal; consistent with your doctrine (you only ask for restraint on uncontrolled systems).
- **Trigger:** if the chair opens with a crisis scenario, or if debate stalls on "urgency vs process."

### FA-4: Human-rights preambular strengthening (Brazil/Algeria price)
- **Target:** PP6.
- **Text (replacement):** *"Affirming that international law, including international humanitarian law and international human rights law, in particular the right to life, applies to all weapons systems, and reaffirming that any weapon which cannot be used in compliance with international humanitarian law must not be used;"*
- **Purpose:** Brazil + Algeria + maximalists' price; the right-to-life framing is Algeria's exact language (May 2025).
- **Trigger:** during merger talks, offer this as the olive branch.

## B. DEFENSIVE AMENDMENTS (against RIVAL text / hostile amendments)

### DA-1: The "voluntary" injection killer
- **Situation:** rival moves to insert "voluntary" before "instrument" in your OP1.
- **Counter-amendment:** *"Recognizing that voluntary and national measures contribute to responsible military use of artificial intelligence, the General Assembly affirms, in this regard, the urgent need for a legally binding international instrument as set out in operative paragraph 1;"*
- **Effect:** converts their poison into a preambular acknowledgment; OP1's "legally binding" survives.
- **Line:** "The delegate's own amendment admits what the preamble now states — voluntary measures are complementary, not substitutes. Let us vote on the substance."

### DA-2: The "existing intergovernmental process" swap
- **Situation:** rival moves to replace OP2's negotiation-mandate language with Pact-for-the-Future wording ("advance with urgency discussions through the existing intergovernmental process").
- **Counter-amendment:** keep OP2, add: *"Nothing in the present resolution shall be construed as prejudging the rules of procedure of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons or the prerogatives of its High Contracting Parties;"*
- **Effect:** removes their procedural objection while keeping the mandate.
- **Line:** "We are not overriding Geneva's rules; we are asking Geneva to act under them. The amendment the delegation proposes would convert a mandate into a suggestion."

### DA-3: The definition-stall killer
- **Situation:** rival moves to delete OP4 (definitional work) citing "no agreed definition."
- **Counter-amendment:** replace with: *"Requests the Secretary-General to compile existing national and multilateral definitions and implementation practices regarding human control over weapons systems and to transmit them to Member States to inform the negotiation process;"*
- **Effect:** keeps the work, moves it to a process — kills the "too hard to define" objection.
- **Line:** "The delegate who cannot define human control should explain how their own armed forces practice it. We are not inventing a definition; we are collecting the world's."

### DA-4: The scope-expansion trap
- **Situation:** maximalist amendment expands OP1 to "all autonomous systems, including surveillance and logistics."
- **Counter:** invoke OP10 + move to divide the question (vote on the expansion separately).
- **Line:** "This amendment would ban the logistics, surveillance and cybersecurity systems every delegation here — including its sponsor — uses daily. It is not a prohibition; it is a self-inflicted wound. The two-tier structure already prohibits what must be prohibited; the savings clause protects what must be protected."

### DA-5: The "no new UN body" budget objection
- **Situation:** rival objects that OP9 (capacity program) costs money / creates bureaucracy.
- **Counter-amendment:** *"...within existing resources and mandates of the United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs and the United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research, and through voluntary contributions;"*
- **Effect:** kills the funding objection without killing the program.
- **Line:** "Capacity building is the cheapest clause in this resolution and the most expensive omission in its absence."

### DA-6: Anti-singling-out shield (your red line #1)
- **Situation:** any amendment or speech targeting Morocco's territorial integrity, or adding singling-out language against any state.
- **Counter:** point of order (relevance to agenda) + one right of reply, then ignore.
- **Line (right of reply):** "The distinguished delegate has confused this committee's agenda with another dossier. We are here to decide whether machines may kill without human judgment. Morocco's position is on the record; the floor is better used on the agenda."

## D. PROCEDURAL WEAPONS NOTE
- **Motion to divide the question:** your best procedural tool against a poisoned amalgam — split OP1/OP2 from contested clauses, force separate recorded votes.
- **Order of the day / agenda:** keep the agenda as-is (the guide's agenda is your advantage — "regulations regarding LAWS" supports the two-tier framing).
- **Right of reply discipline:** max 2 uses, never on substance, always on personal attacks.
- **Voting:** if your conference uses simple majority — your 120+ bloc is unbreakable; if 2/3 required — still unbreakable. Recorded votes on contested OPs lock in the narrative for the chair's memory.
