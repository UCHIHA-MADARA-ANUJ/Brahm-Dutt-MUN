Russia says definitions are unclear — so let us negotiate them, as the GGE's own characterization already shows we can. 'The definition is unclear' is an argument for negotiations, not against them."

## 4. ICRC MARCH 2026 FRAMEWORK (verbatim concepts — your OP1's two-tier authority)
- Prohibit: (a) **unpredictable AWS** (functioning/effects cannot be sufficiently understood, predicted or explained); (b) **anti-personnel AWS** (designed or used to apply force against persons).
- Regulate all others via: limits on **type of targets**; limits on **duration, scale, geographic scope**; limits on **situations of use** (no civilians present); requirements for **effective human supervision, timely intervention, deactivation** (or self-destruction/neutralization).
- **Debate line:** "When the ICRC — the guardian of the Geneva Conventions — describes prohibition and regulation in the same sentence, the two-tier approach is not a Moroccan invention. It is the consensus of the humanitarian legal profession."

## 5. GGE CHAIR'S APRIL 2026 REPORT (supervision elements — your OP4/OP6 detail)
"Context-appropriate human judgement and control is needed to ensure the use and effects of [AWS] are in compliance with international law." Supervision = responsible **chain of command and control**; effects **adequately anticipated and controlled**; **mission parameters cannot be significantly modified** by the system; **deactivation in a timely manner**; operation limited to a **defined perimeter**.
- **Debate line:** "The GGE has already defined the operational content of human control. The rolling text is not a blank page; it is a draft waiting for a signature."
